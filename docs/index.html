Scan-and-Done — in-room QR to direct booking

A guest scans the card in their room. No app, no login, no typing. The page knows which room it is, looks up who is checked in right now, reads the rate they paid the OTA, and offers a lower direct price they can pay in a few taps.

Try it

Open these on a phone if you can — that is what a guest holds. Nothing is charged; the payment step is a sandbox. These links always work: if the full server is running they open it, and if it is not they open an always-on preview that runs the same pricing code in your browser.
